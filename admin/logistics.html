🚕 Rideshare

Driving in? Post your route and seats. Need a lift? Claim one.

How it works
  • Drivers: click Set up your ride or + Offer a ride and enter the real starting point for the trip to the event and the real destination after the event (these can be different — e.g. you live in Berlin but drive home via Amsterdam).
  • Dates: arrival and departure are pulled from your My Info panel above. To change them, edit them there — they update everywhere else automatically.
  • Passengers: claim a seat on the leg you need (to event, from event, or both). It's just a signal — message the driver directly to confirm pickup time, fuel contribution, luggage etc.
  • Can't find a ride here? Try the wider community: Discord #rideshare channel or the community rideshare spreadsheet.
